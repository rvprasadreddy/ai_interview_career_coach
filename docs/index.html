Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the AI Interviewer and our preparation platform.

How does the adaptive voice practice work?
InterviPrep uses high-fidelity Speech-to-Text translation combined with advanced context memory. When you speak your answers, our AI instantly transcripts the text, evaluates your alignment with target competencies, and dynamically constructs highly realistic follow-up questions just like a professional human recruiter.
Can I customize the interviewer's personality?
Yes! You can configure the simulator behavior completely. Choose between a supportive/encouraging style, a standard professional HR agent, or an aggressive "stress-test" challenger personality designed to prepare you for highly technical or senior management roles.
How does the ATS score calculator help me?
Our ATS (Applicant Tracking System) optimizer matches your specific resume bullets against the exact requirements of your target job description. It identifies keyword deficiencies, analyzes semantic matching, and suggests precise revisions to ensure you pass automated resume filters and secure direct interviews.
Is there a free trial session available?
Absolutely! You can start a completely free session directly from the web landing page. Downloading the official mobile app unlocks deeper analytical breakdowns, historical tracking, daily drills, and unlimited document reviews.
