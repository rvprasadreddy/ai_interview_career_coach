# InterviPrep User Operational Guide

This guide provides detailed instructions on how to use the InterviPrep application to master your interview skills, optimize your resume, and build a consistent practice habit.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Account Creation & Login
InterviPrep offers multiple secure ways to access your account:

*   **LinkedIn Sync (Recommended)**: 
    *   Tap "Continue with LinkedIn" on the login screen.
    *   Authorize the app to automatically sync your professional headline, experience, and skills.
    *   This is the fastest way to get a personalized experience.
*   **Email & Password**:
    *   Sign up with your email and a strong password.
    *   Use the "Forgot Password" link if you need to reset credentials.
*   **Passwordless OTP**:
    *   Enter your email to receive a secure One-Time Password for quick access without remembering complicated passwords.

### 2. Onboarding & Profile Setup
Your first login will guide you through a unified setup process:

1.  **Basic Identity**: Confirm your name, email, and phone number.
2.  **Location**: The app auto-detects your city/country, but you can update your "Preferred Location" for job targeting.
3.  **Resume Upload**: Upload your resume (PDF or DOCX). The AI will immediately analyze it to tailor interview questions to your background.
4.  **Career Goals**: Select your target roles (e.g., "Senior Product Manager", "Flutter Developer") to customize your Daily Drills.

## 🏠 Home Screen & Dashboard

The Home Screen is your centralized command center, designed to give you an immediate snapshot of your career readiness.

### 1. Interview Readiness Roadmap
The core of the dashboard is the **Readiness Score**, a dynamic percentage that combines your interview performance and resume quality into a single "Hireability Index."
*   **Target**: Aim for **85%+** to be considered "Job Ready."
*   **Visualizations**: toggle between two views:
    *   **Skill Bar**: A linear breakdown of your competence in each category.
    *   **Skill Radar**: A hexagonal spider chart that visualizes your balance across:
        *   *Communication* (Clarity, Pacing)
        *   *Technical* (Domain Knowledge)
        *   *Behavioral* (STAR Method)
        *   *Problem Solving* (Analytical Thinking)
        *   *Resume & ATS* (Document Quality)

### 2. Daily Prep Zone
A quick-access card showing your **Daily Drill** status.
*   See today's question preview.
*   Track your current **Streak** (represented by the 🔥 icon).
*   Jump straight into practice with one tap.

### 3. Interview History Snapshot
Your most recent interview sessions are listed here for quick review.
*   **Status Badges**: Quickly identify the state of each session:
    *   🟢 **Completed**: Full report available.
    *   🟠 **In Progress**: Can be resumed (if supported) or reviewed.
    *   ⚪ **Abandoned**: Sessions that were stopped early.
*   **Detailed Report Access**: Tap any "Completed" interview to view the full executive summary, transcript, and audio playback.

## 🎙️ Mock Interview Simulator

Experience a realistic interview environment with our AI.

### Setting Up a Session
1.  **Target Role**: Choose the specific job title you are practicing for.
2.  **Difficulty Level**: Select from *Junior, Mid-Level, Senior, Lead,* or *Executive*.
3.  **AI Personality**:
    *   **Friendly**: Good for warming up and building confidence.
    *   **Professional**: Standard corporate tone.
    *   **Challenging**: tough follow-ups and critical feedback (Best for stress testing).
4.  **Configuration**: Set the number of questions (3-10) and enable/disable specific question types.

### Post-Interview Review
After finishing a session, you receive a comprehensive **Detailed Interview Report**:
*   **Executive Summary**: An "Interviewer Closure Note" giving a human-like hiring verdict (e.g., "Strong Hire", "Maybe").
*   **Score Breakdown**: See exactly how you scored on *Structure*, *Depth*, *Clarity*, and *Relevance*.
*   **Full Transcript**: Read through every question and your answers.
*   **Audio Replay**: Listen to your recorded answers to self-critique your delivery.

## 🔥 Daily Drill

Build a consistent habit with one high-impact question every day.

*   **The Drill**: You receive ONE curated question at 8:00 AM tailored to your weakest skill area.
*   **Ideal Answer**: Stuck? Tap "Reveal Ideal Answer" to see a model response structured with best practices (e.g., STAR method).
*   **Streak System**:
    *   🔥 **Active Streak**: Answer daily to keep the fire burning.
    *   🏆 **Best Streak**: Track your all-time record.
    *   Missing a day resets your active streak to zero!
*   **Confidence Rating**: After answering, rate yourself (1-5 stars) to help the algorithm adjust future difficulty.
*   **Notifications**: Smart reminders ensure you never break the chain.

## 🧠 Practice Hub

Your personalized AI learning library. The Practice Hub analyzes your past interviews to create a custom curriculum.

### 1. Mastery Path
Visualizes your journey through 4 stages of competence:
*   **Foundation** → **Intermediate** → **Advanced** → **Expert**.
*   Track your progress bar to the next milestone.

### 2. Strength & Improvement Analysis
The AI identifies patterns across all your interviews:
*   **Strongest Areas**: The top 3 categories where you consistently perform well (Green cards).
*   **Areas to Improve**: The bottom 3 categories dragging down your score (Orange cards). Focus your effort here!

### 3. Expert View (AI Recommendations)
Based on your "Areas to Improve," the Hub dynamically curates:
*   **Video Lessons**: High-quality YouTube tutorials from industry experts.
*   **Blogs & Articles**: In-depth reading on specific concepts.
*   **FAQs**: Common questions and answers for your target role.
*   **Learning Impact**: Completing these resources directly boosts your backend "Readiness Score."

## 📄 Resume Analysis (ATS)

Ensure your resume beats the Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

### 1. ATS Score & Match Rate
*   **Score**: A 0-100 rating indicating how well your resume parses.
*   **Target Role**: The analysis is specific to the role you set in your profile (e.g., "Product Manager").

### 2. AI Improvement Suggestions
Tap "Improve Resume" to get a detailed breakdown:
*   **Suggestions List**: Specific, actionable edits (e.g., "Add 'Agile Methodologies' to Skills section").
*   **Impact Label**: Each suggestion is tagged with **High**, **Medium**, or **Low** impact.
*   **Type**:
    *   **AMEND**: Change existing text.
    *   **ADD**: Missing information that should be included.
*   **Relevance Score**: How confident the AI is that this change will help.

### 3. Detailed Insights
*   **Strengths**: What you are doing right (keep these!).
*   **Skill Extraction**: See exactly which keywords the AI found. If a key skill isn't listed here, the bots can't see it either!

## ❓ FAQ & Troubleshooting

**Q: My microphone isn't working.**
*   A: Ensure you have granted Microphone permissions to InterviPrep in your device settings.

**Q: How is the 'Readiness Score' calculated?**
*   A: It is a weighted average of your Interview Performance (60%) and Resume ATS Score (40%), adjusted by your Daily Drill consistency.

**Q: Can I re-take a Daily Drill?**
*   A: No, the Daily Drill is a one-shot opportunity to simulate real pressure. You can review past drills in the History tab.

**Q: Why don't I see the 'Reveal Answer' button?**
*   A: This feature is available after you attempt to answer or if you choose to "View Answer" directly (which may not count towards your perfect streak depending on settings).
