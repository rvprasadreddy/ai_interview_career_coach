# InterviPrep: Product Features for Website Building

This document outlines the core features of **InterviPrep**, structured for high-impact presentation on a marketing landing page or website.

---

## 🚀 1. AI-Powered Mock Interview Simulator
*Prepare for the real thing with our state-of-the-art AI interviewer.*

- **Realistic Voice Interaction**: Experience organic, hands-free interviews with native Text-to-Speech (TTS) and Speech-to-Text (STT) orchestration.
- **Dynamic Follow-up Questions**: Our AI doesn't just read a list; it listens to your answers and asks targeted follow-ups just like a human recruiter.
- **Adjustable AI Personalities**: Choose your interviewer's vibe—from **Professional** and **Friendly** to **Challenging**—to prep for any interview culture.
- **Customizable Scenarios**: Target specific roles and companies with difficulty levels ranging from Junior to Lead.
- **Reactive UI Visuals**: Real-time voice visualizers and AI status indicators (Thinking, Speaking, Listening) provide a premium, immersive experience.

## 🔥 2. Daily Drill (Consistent Habit Building)
*Master the art of interviewing, one day at a time.*


- **One Question a Day**: Stay sharp with a daily curated interview question tailored to your weak areas.
- **Smart Notification System**: Get personalized reminders at 8 AM and 11 AM based on your streak and readiness, ensuring you never break a habit.
- **Deep Linking**: One tap from the notification takes you straight to the action, frictionless and fast.
- **Daily Streaks**: Build momentum and track your consistency with our integrated streak system.
- **Confidence Tracking**: Rate how you felt about each answer to see your self-assurance grow over time.
- **Smart Progress Analytics**: Watch your "Readiness Score" climb as you complete your daily drills.
- **Celebratory Milestones**: Interactive celebrations and confetti to reward your dedication.

## 📄 3. Intelligent Resume Analysis (ATS Optimizer)
*Get pass the bots and land the interview.*

- **Instant ATS Scoring**: Upload your resume (PDF/Docx) and get an immediate score based on industry-standard Applicant Tracking Systems.
- **Actionable AI Insights**: Get a detailed breakdown of your resume's strengths and specific fixes to improve your hireability.
- **Skill Extraction**: Automatically identify and highlight your core competencies to align with your career goals.
- **Cloud-Synced Profile**: Keep your professional identity updated across all devices with our secure cloud storage.

## 📊 4. Advanced Analytics & Performance Radar
*Data-driven insights to polish your performance.*

- **Interactive Radar Charts**: Visualize your performance across 6 key dimensions: Relevance, Structure, Technical Accuracy, Depth, Communication, and Confidence.
- **"Actual vs. Target" Benchmarking**: Compare your current skills against a professional benchmark (85% target) using our premium glassmorphic radar charts with gradient fills.
- **Executive Summary Reports**: Receive a comprehensive "Interviewer Closure Note" and hiring recommendation (Strong Hire, Hire, Maybe, No Hire) after every session.
- **Detailed History**: Review every past interview with full transcripts, category-specific scoring, and growth trends.

## 🧠 5. Practice Lab & Adaptive Learning
*Your personalized roadmap to career success.*

- **Adaptive Learning Journey**: A high-fidelity vertical timeline representing 5 preparation stages: Foundation, Deep Dive, Simulation, High Stakes, and Negotiation.
- **Dynamic Stage Focus**: Smart cards that highlight current progress, category strengths/weaknesses (e.g., "Strongest: Behavioral, Weakest: Technical"), and upcoming rewards.
- **Categorized Skill Hubs**: Deep dive into specific question types: Behavioral, Technical, Leadership, Situational, and Problem-Solving.
- **AI-Powered Hints**: Stuck on a question? Get intelligent hints and structural advice to craft the perfect response.
- **Gamified Progress**: Earn XP and unlock achievement badges as you master new skills.

## 🤝 6. Seamless Integrations & Premium UX
*A frictionless experience designed for professionals.*

- **One-Tap LinkedIn Sync**: Sign up and sync your professional profile, skills, and work history instantly with LinkedIn OAuth 2.0.
- **Google-Style Security**: Passwordless Email-OTP and traditional authentication options secured by Supabase.
- **Premium Glassmorphism Design**: A sleek, modern interface with Material 3 styling, immersive `BackdropFilter` blur effects, and smooth animations.
- **Animated Global Sidebar**: A glassmorphic sidebar accessible from any screen for unified settings and profile management.
- **Animated FAB Experience**: Our "Start Interview" button features dual-glow effects, pulsing ring animations, and smooth text expansion.
- **Dark Mode Support**: A classic, eye-friendly dark theme optimized for late-night prep sessions.
- **Offline Resilience**: Practice on the go—your progress is saved locally and synced automatically when you're back online.
